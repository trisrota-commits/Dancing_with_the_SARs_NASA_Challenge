# NASA Space Apps: past Global Winners, for ideas

A summary of the Global Winners from 2022 to 2025: what each team built, which
award it won, and a link to its project page. At the end there are some
patterns that matter for our own project.

Each project page on spaceappschallenge.org has the team's description,
and usually a demo video, slides or a link to the working app. Some apps
from older years may no longer be online.

---

## How judging works

1. **Local judges** pick the best projects at each local event. These become
   **Global Nominees** (over 1,290 in 2025).
2. **NASA and partner-agency experts** review the nominees and choose about
   40 **Global Finalists**, plus Honorable Mentions.
3. **An executive committee** from NASA and partner agencies picks
   **10 Global Winners**, one per award.

Projects are judged on five criteria: **Impact, Creativity, Validity,
Relevance and Presentation**. Global Winners are invited to a NASA centre.

Projects submitted to "Create Your Own Challenge" cannot reach Global Judging.

### The 10 awards

| Award | What it rewards (official wording, shortened) |
|---|---|
| **Best Use of Science** | The most sound and valid use of science or the scientific method |
| **Best Use of Data** | Making space data more accessible, or using it in a distinctive way |
| **Best Use of Technology** | The most innovative use of technology |
| **Galactic Impact** | The greatest potential to improve life on Earth or in the universe |
| **Best Mission Concept** | The most plausible concept and design |
| **Most Inspirational** | Projects that capture people's hearts |
| **Best Use of Storytelling** | The most creative use of narrative to show what open data can do |
| **Global Connection** | Best connects people around the world through technology |
| **Art & Technology** | Best combines technical and creative skills |
| **Local Impact** | The greatest potential for local impact |

## What this means for us

These are observations from the 40 winners above, not official guidance.

### Winners close to what we are building

- **Satellite tracking with "when will it pass over me" won twice.**
  *Landsat Connect* (2024, Best Mission Concept) tracked Landsat and alerted
  users to the next pass. The 2022 ISS tracker in 3D won Global Connection.
  Our live NISAR orbit and next-pass countdown are in the same family, but
  they add radar data and real events on top.
- **Sonification won Global Connection in 2023.** *Arcobaleno* turned NASA
  images into sound. Our sound features and the "Dancing" theme are on a
  proven path. Framing them around accessibility makes them stronger.
- **Earth observation for real users wins often.** Five Earth winners in two
  years: *Waterwise* and *2plant* (farmers and drought, 2024), *G.R.O.W.*
  (wildfire GIS for non-experts, 2024), *SkySense* (weather, 2025) and
  *QUEÑARIS* (forest restoration, 2025). Each had a **specific user** and a
  **decision** it helps with.
- **Immersive 3D visualisation of Earth data won Art & Technology** in 2024
  (*Connected Earth Museum*).

### Patterns across the winners

1. **A clear person who benefits.** Farmers, children, people with sight
   impairments, a city in Peru. "Everyone" is weaker than "a flood
   responder in Bangladesh".
2. **Science that holds up.** The science and data awards went to models
   and analyses with real, checkable results (the LSTM storm forecast, the
   neural-network quake detector). Our calibrated NISAR measurements matter
   most here.
3. **A story, not just a tool.** Storytelling, Inspirational and Art awards
   went to games, comics, children's stories and a museum. A short narrative
   through our app ("follow the flood the antelopes follow") could compete
   here.
4. **A local angle.** Local Impact winners focused on one real place. Our
   glaciers, the West Bengal rice fields or the Sudd could each anchor that.
5. **Polish is not enough on its own.** Most winners did one thing deeply,
   rather than many things on the surface.

### Awards that fit our project best

| Award | Why we fit | What would strengthen it |
|---|---|---|
| **Best Use of Data** | Live NISAR, events, wildlife and measurements in one place | Show calibrated results, not only previews |
| **Global Connection** | Global, live, and sound for accessibility | Frame the sound around people who cannot see the screen |
| **Art & Technology** | Live sky, moving satellite, sound | Keep decorations clearly labelled |
| **Best Use of Science** | Calibrated dB time series and change maps | Run the measurements and explain one result well |
| **Local Impact** | Places like West Bengal rice or the Sudd | Pick one place and one user and go deep |
