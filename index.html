Dancing with the SARs

Earth's changing surface, seen by the NISAR radar satellite

Stories: start here

Five places, five kinds of change, each explained from NISAR data.

Events on the map

Bigger dot = stronger event, and in the 3D view a taller beam means the same. A white ring means NISAR imaged that spot in its latest passes. Click a dot to see the event and what NISAR saw there.

Changing places

    Diamonds mark slow changes that no event feed reports: glacier loss, sinking cities, wetland floods, forest clearing, farming seasons, volcanoes, moving slopes and a filling reservoir. Click one to play NISAR’s passes over it.

    NISAR radar
    • Green: forest and rough ground (strong HV echo)
    • Magenta: bare ground and buildings (strong HH echo)
    • Dark: calm water, which reflects the pulse away
    • Grey picture: a pass recorded in one polarisation only
    Live sky and wildlife

    Real sighting records, 2010–2025, played by month of the year. They also show where people look, so busy coasts have more.

    The satellite, sky, sunlight and sightings are real data. Shooting stars are decoration only.
